## When do Target Black Friday sales actually start?

Target runs its holiday discounts in three overlapping waves. The confirmed schedule, from Target's own announcement:

- **Deals of the Day:** November 1 through December 24, with a fresh set of discounts every single day
- **Early Black Friday sale:** November 5 through December 7
- **Limited-time weekend offers** sprinkled throughout the holiday season, with Target hinting at another early event ahead of the proper Black Friday push

Black Friday itself falls on **Friday, November 27**, the day after Thanksgiving (November 26), with Cyber Monday landing on **Monday, November 30**. Deals continue around those dates, but the deepest one-day price cuts tend to cluster near the peak weekend.

The practical implication: big-ticket categories usually hit their first serious markdowns during the November 5–December 7 window. If the item you want drops early, Target's 14-day price match (covered below) protects you from feeling foolish if the price dips again on November 27.

## How Target structures the discounts

Target doesn't run one uniform sale. Three mechanisms carry the season, and knowing which one holds the deal you want saves a lot of refreshing.

**Weekly Ad deals** are the backbone. Target publishes its Black Friday pricing week by week in the Weekly Ad on Target.com and in the Target app, and that's where most category-level discounts live. If you want the exact dates for a specific deal, this is the official source.

**Target Circle offers** are clipped to your free Circle account and applied at checkout. Circle membership is free, takes a minute to set up, and comes with a birthday perk — a 5% discount redeemable through the app on your birthday. During Target's Circle Week events (which run roughly three times a year, typically in March, July, and October), discounts reach up to 50% with some buy-one-get-one offers. Black Friday is a bigger stage than Circle Week, and Circle members tend to get first or exclusive access to portions of it.

**Deals of the Day** rotate daily from November 1, and they're where Target parks its attention-grabbing prices. From the official announcement, the headline teaser prices include:

- 100% cashmere (excluding functional trim) starting at **$25**
- Pajamas for all from **$10**
- Festive decor from **$5**
- Faux fur throws at **$30**
- "Thousands of gifts priced at $5 and under"

Top toys from LEGO, Barbie, and Nintendo are flagged as focus categories, along with exclusive beauty gift sets at Target Beauty Studio and nostalgic treats from Target's Favorite Day line. TopCashback's own Target page describes Black Friday at Target as one of its biggest sale events of the year, with beauty, clothing, shoes, artificial Christmas trees, and home decor hitting up to 50% off, and Cyber Monday continuing the run on lighting, small appliances, furniture, and beauty.

One protection worth knowing before you buy anything early: Target matches its own prices for **14 days**. Buy a TV on November 20, watch it drop on November 27, and you can claim the difference. Note the limit — it only matches Target's own lower prices, so it won't help if Walmart undercuts Target. Comparing prices across retailers before buying is still on you.
